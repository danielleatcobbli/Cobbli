Before/after photos for "Coming soon" services
================================================

Drop photos in this folder. Same convention as the regular services photos
(public/condition-photos/): lowercase, dashes instead of spaces, and a
"-before" / "-after" suffix. Any common image format works (.jpg, .jpeg, .png,
.webp).

Once a pair of files lands here, tell me and I'll wire them in — no need to
touch any code yourself. A service only needs a "-before" photo to show up;
the "-after" is optional (hovering just won't reveal anything until it's
added).
